Traders operating near the Kakarvitta crossing say freight volume has picked up noticeably over the past two weeks, a pattern local business associations describe as typical ahead of the festival season as retailers restock inventory.

The increase has meant longer processing times at peak hours, though traders say the recently extended customs service window has helped absorb some of the additional load. Businesses are advising customers to expect standard seasonal delays for cross-border shipments through the coming weeks.
